## Decisions taken during the build (23-10-2026)
- TypeScript 7 is kept, and oxlint is the linter (ESLint is not used).
- valibot is the schema library.
- A mode needs at least one runway end across its arrivals and departures.
- No enforced minimum poll interval; the lower bound is 1 second.
- A whole-day time band needs `allDay` set to true.
- The window choices on screen are 4 hours, 6 hours and the full TAF validity, default the full TAF; the 2-hour option is dropped (OD-31).
- The traffic panel shows no trend; the raw METAR and TAF are shown at the top, and Panel C shows components against limits only (OD-32).
- Variable wind is not judged against the wet tailwind limit; above calm it is MARGINAL, and UNSUITABLE only if the full speed breaks the dry tailwind limit or the crosswind limit on every runway of the mode (OD-33).
- Weather in the vicinity (VC) never counts as precipitation or a wet runway (OD-34).
